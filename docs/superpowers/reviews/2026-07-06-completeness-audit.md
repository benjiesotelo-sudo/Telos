**The cross-cutting verdict device (V).** No builder anywhere in `src/lib/results/` renders a plain-language met/violated assumption verdict sentence; every card appends live assumption statistics (Levene F/p, Shapiro W/p, Mauchly, Box's M) to a static note and leaves the judgment to prose.
